II How we work

We build your home with you.

Most builders hand the drawing to someone else to price, and hand the price to someone else to build. We keep design in the house. Our design team listens to what matters to you and walks the lot to learn its layout and restrictions, local Lowcountry architects draw the plan with us, and the team that will build it prices it, so nothing is lost between the sketch and the site.

The budget is established early, before the plans are finalised, with an up-front account of what each feature adds, so change orders are the exception rather than the business model. Because a house is hard to see on paper, the lead designer builds an immersive 3D model at the same time as the preliminary plans, approved by a structural engineer, and you tour every room, fixture and finish before construction begins.

III Our partner in Charlotte

What the partnership brings to Charleston.

We work in partnership with RAM Construction of Charlotte, which has built custom homes there since 2004: 6,000-square-foot modern homes in SouthPark, early-century Craftsman bungalows in Dilworth, and the renovations that keep an old street whole. It is a regular on the Dilworth Home Tour.

Charleston draws on that experience directly: the design and 3D modelling tools, the fixed-bid process with every allowance listed, the weekly site meeting and written recap, and the standard of finish in the photographs across this site. RAM Construction Charleston holds its own South Carolina license and builds with its own Lowcountry team.
