How we build. From the lot to the keys.

Four steps, one project manager, a site meeting every week and a written recap after each one. Here is what happens, in the order it happens.

  • Design
  • Fixed bid
  • 3D walk-through
  • Build

I The four steps. What happens, in order.

Each step ends with something you can hold: a drawing, a number, a model you have walked through, a set of keys.

i

Design

We walk the lot and read what it allows. Then our design team and local Lowcountry architects draw the plans, elevations and materials with you over the table, and the budget takes shape beside the drawing so you decide what to keep with the number in front of you.

ii

Fixed bid

The team that will build it prices the drawing. Every allowance is listed line by line and the bid is fixed before you sign, so change orders are the exception rather than the business model.

iii

Walk-through

Our lead designer builds an immersive 3D model alongside the preliminary plans, approved by a structural engineer. You tour every room, fixture and finish and change what you want to change on the model, not on site.

iv

Build

A dedicated RAM Charleston team builds the home you approved. One project manager from first sketch to final walk-through, a site meeting every week, a written recap after each one, and a schedule you can see.

II 3D home walk-through. Stand in the room before it exists.

RAM's immersive 3D plans let you walk every room of the proposed design before it exists: the view from the kitchen, the evening sun on the porch floor, every fixture and finish. You review the home before we build it, and change what you want to change on the model, not on site.

Questions / before you call

How is the price set?
After design, we issue a fixed bid with allowances listed line by line, so you know the number before you sign.
When do we talk about budget?
Early, before the plans are final. We explain what each feature adds to the total as we design, so you decide what to keep with the number in front of you rather than after a bid comes back.
What is the 3D walk-through?
Our lead designer builds an immersive 3D model alongside the preliminary plans, so you tour every room, fixture and finish before construction. The model is approved by a structural engineer, so the changes you make in it can be trusted.
